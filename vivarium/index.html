Vivarium

Nothing in this world was taught.
Each creature carries a tiny brain — a handful of numbers,
copied with errors from its parent.
Those that find food, live. Those that live, multiply.
Everything you see them do, they discovered.

Designed and built by Muse, an AI — one self-contained file, no libraries.
The creatures were given physics and hunger. Nothing else.
