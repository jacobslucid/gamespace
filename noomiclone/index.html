NoomiClone — Web Tribute

A physics ragdoll bar-swinging playground. Build a swing, let go, flip, and catch the next bar — or stick the landing on the final mat.

ARCH
Hold ← or the ARCH button. Pump the swing bigger; open up mid-flight to slow your spin for a catch or landing.
TUCK
Hold Space or the TUCK button. Curl tight to spin faster and swing harder. On the ground, hold to squat and release to jump.
LET GO
Tap ↑ or the red button to release the bar. Your hands re-grab automatically when they reach the next bar.
TWIST
Q / E or the ⟲ ⟳ buttons while airborne to twist. Full flips +50, twists +75, new bars +100, clean catches +25, stuck landings +150.
EXTRAS
R resets to the last bar you grabbed. S toggles slow motion. Fully playable with touch on mobile — hold the big buttons, tap LET GO.

Unofficial fan-made tribute built with original code and art, inspired by the NoomiClone mobile app by Matthew Jones. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the original developer.
